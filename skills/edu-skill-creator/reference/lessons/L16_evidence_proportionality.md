## L16 — Evidence burden scales with a claim's specificity, consequence and volatility

**Rule.** Precise, consequential or time-sensitive claims require correspondingly precise and current
support. Low-stakes illustrative claims are not burdened with irrelevant precision. And a precision
claim is never laundered through a permissive category tag: calling something an anecdote does not
exempt the number inside it.

**Failure that taught it.** A teaching story shipped through four review gates carrying an inverted
premise and a specific percentage. The faculty pushback — that classroom stories are told to hold
attention, and that students may not scrutinise the underlying statistics — came from the same person
who had caught the inversion at a gate. That is why the doctrine became proportionality rather than
exemption: the argument for relaxing scrutiny was made by the only party who had actually applied it.

**Enforcement.** `edu-skill-creator-draft` requires each criterion's evidence burden to be stated
alongside it, and `edu-skill-creator-test` scenario 16 seeds a precision claim inside a permissive
category tag and checks that the pipeline still demands support for it.

**Related folds.** Corpus provenance lives in L1 (state what a derived corpus is an artifact of
before deriving from it); invented-threshold discipline lives in L11 (prefer a presence check or a
reported value); the decay of world-dependent examples lives in L9 (date the story, assign a named
owner to re-verify before each live use, with a fallback).

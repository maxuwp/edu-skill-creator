# Grounding Library Audit — Scope, Currency, Citation Truth

**Subject:** the 30 framework anchors in `edu_grounding_library.md` · **Date:** 2026-07-31
**Method:** Half A = pointed, single-source scope/numeric verification (one research pass per cluster, quote + URL required for every non-`unverified` verdict). Half B = open deep-research pass per cluster on what has changed/been contested/superseded since publication. Anchor 30 (Ma, ASEE 2026) is the requesting author's own unpublished paper — skipped per instruction, marked `author-set`.

No anchor came back fully `unverified` — a source with an accessible quote was found for all 29 examinable anchors, though several numeric sub-claims within otherwise-verified anchors are separately flagged as untraceable (noted inline).

| 3 | Bloom's taxonomy, revised (Anderson & Krathwohl, 2001) | scope-accurate | Two-dimensional **classification** (Knowledge × Cognitive Process). Treat "level order = teaching order" as actively contested, not just non-authoritative. |
| 4 | Gagné's nine events of instruction | scope-accurate | Scoped explicitly to events supporting **one performance objective within a single lesson**; order is stated as approximate and not all events are required every lesson. Not superseded, not fading. |
| 5 | Merrill's first principles | **superseded (cite newer edition)** | 2002 paper is a synthesis/"preliminary report" of prescriptive design principles abstracted from prior theories (Gagné included); no empirical population tested, no effect sizes. Citing the 2002 paper alone is out of date; recommend re-pointing the anchor to the 2020/2024 edition. |
| 6 | Ausubel (advance organizers) | **scope-too-broad** | The 1960 study tested prose organizers before **unfamiliar written expository text** with university students — a reading/text-comprehension manipulation, not a general lesson-opener method. Full text is paywalled (APA); only the first-party abstract was accessible, so method details (n=120, 500-word organizer, 3-day-delay test) are secondary-sourced from a dissertation quoting Ausubel. | "The purpose of this study is to test the hypothesis that the learning and retention of unfamiliar but meaningful verbal material can be facilitated by the advance introduction of relevant subsuming concepts (organizers)." | [Ausubel 1960, abstract](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/The-use-of-advance-organizers-in-the-learning-and-Ausubel/aae103c3783d102069805e9d6a1c0d61c8c49de4) | No new edition, but standing has shifted materially: subsequent meta-analyses range g=0.21–0.89 (pooled ≈0.42), and Bryce & Blown (2023, *Current Psychology*) argue Ausubel's static representational cognitive model conflicts with contemporary memory research. Treat as historically important but empirically contested, not settled. |

| 7 | Cognitive Load Theory (Sweller) | **scope-too-broad** | Founding paper = small experiments + a computational model on Australian Year-10 (15–16 yo) students solving well-structured trigonometry/kinematics/geometry problems; object of study is problem-format design for schema acquisition — not instructional materials generally. Measurement validity remains contested: de Jong (2010) found "the critical level indicating overload is unknown"; a 2026 ACM ITiCSE study found neither of two multi-item load surveys predicted performance; falsifiability critiques continued into 2026 (Kohn, Leppink, Sortwell). | 8 | ICAP (Chi & Wylie) | scope-accurate | Classification of overt engagement behaviors, explicitly orthogonal to content selection. ## What would we retire, add, or re-scope

**Retire or replace the citation, not the concept:**
